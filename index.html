Can Alem move money today?

Payment execution is the next stage of the roadmap. Finance currently supports invoices, approvals and payment matching; Agent Gateway runs as a private-beta sandbox. Live bank, card and stablecoin settlement is not connected.

Who is Alem built for?

Finance teams can start with invoice workflows today. We welcome banks, payment providers and AI product teams as design partners for controlled agent payments.

Do we have to replace our accounting software?

No. Alem is designed to work alongside your accounting process. Start with supported imports and review workflows; discuss specific integrations and their availability during onboarding.
