    Why we start with invoices

    A financial action,
    with context.

    Whether requested by a person or an AI agent, every business payment needs clear context, explicit rules and a traceable record before money moves.

    Understand the obligation.

    The supplier, invoice or request, amount, due date and supporting context establish why a payment is due.

    Apply company rules.

    Role permissions, spending limits and human approvals establish who may act and when a person must decide.

    Keep a traceable record.

    Connect the request and approval to the payment record. Reconciliation shows what was paid and what remains open.

    Why invoices first? The invoice establishes the obligation, approval establishes authority, and reconciliation closes the loop. These are the records an agent will need to act responsibly.

    A few practical questions
    Can Alem move money today?

    Not yet. Finance supports invoices, approvals and reconciliation. Agent Gateway is a private-beta sandbox. Live bank, card and stablecoin payment execution is the next stage, via partners.

    Does the interactive demo execute a payment?

    No. It evaluates three illustrative scenarios locally in your browser. The decision log is temporary and is not a production audit record. No request is sent to a bank or payment provider.

    Do we have to replace our accounting software?

    No. Alem is designed to work alongside your accounting process. Start with supported imports and review workflows; discuss specific integrations and their availability during onboarding.

    Who can become a design partner?

    We welcome banks, payment providers and AI product teams with a concrete business-payment use case. Contact us to discuss the workflow, controls and integration needs.
