Common questions

Before you get started.

Do we need to replace Zoho Books or Wafeq?

No. Alem is designed to sit alongside your accounting workflow. Integration functionality is being validated with pilot users.

Can we invite the whole finance team?

Alem Finance supports workspace-based accounts and approvals. Team access and available permissions can be discussed for your pilot.

Does Alem automatically move money?

No. Payment execution and bank transfers are not connected in the current Finance workspace.

Is AI extraction fully automated?

AI-assisted extraction is part of the early-access workflow. Accuracy and availability depend on document type and configured services; human review remains important.

How can we try Finance?

Open the workspace to create an account, or contact the founder for a guided pilot and onboarding help.
